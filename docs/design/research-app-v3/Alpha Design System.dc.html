Alpha PR Labs · Research app
Design language v3 · Sep 2026

A bench instrument for your own research.

The app reads like lab equipment you trust: one reading at a time, printed large, with its scale drawn next to it. Graduated scales replace rings everywhere. The syringe barrel, the days of a cycle, the check-in and a vial's contents all show their units, so a number is never ambiguous. Each screen has one inverted block, and that is where the day's key number lives.

01
One reading, printed large

Today opens on the next dose in syringe units at 88 px. Everything else is smaller and quieter.

02
Every number carries its scale

Rulers with real graduations: 2-unit lines on a 100-unit syringe, one tick per cycle day.

03
State is a shape and a word

Done, due, overdue and low each have a glyph and a label. Colour reinforces; it never carries meaning alone.

04
Light by day, dark by system

Bench-paper light is the default: clinical, and legible at a 7:30 AM dose. Dark follows iOS and inverts the same tokens.

Rules for numbers

Units are written out, lower case, in monomg · mcg · mL · units Never µg (misread as mg), never "u"250 µg · 5u Always a leading zero0.5 mL No trailing zeros on doses2.5 mg Syringe units always name the syringe50 units · 100-unit Units are never rounded to a line; the ruler shows where it falls7.5 units Money: two decimals and the currency$3,925.00 CAD Foreign amounts show the sourceUS$ 460.00 Stacked numbers use tabular figurestnum

The mcg and zero rules follow standard medication-safety practice for dose labelling.
