# Supportive Compounds

# MT-1 (Melanotan 1 / Afamelanotide) 

## What It Is

Melanotan 1 (MT-1), also known as afamelanotide, is a synthetic analog of alpha-melanocyte stimulating hormone (α-MSH) that stimulates melanin production in the skin. Unlike Melanotan 2, MT-1 is more selective for the melanocortin 1 receptor (MC1R) and has achieved FDA approval for treating a rare photosensitivity disorder.

MT-1 was developed at the University of Arizona in the 1980s as part of research into sunless tanning agents. Scientists knew that α-MSH triggered melanogenesis (the process by which skin produces melanin), but the natural hormone was too unstable for practical use. MT-1 was formulated to be up to 26 times more potent and significantly more stable than natural α-MSH.

In October 2019, the FDA approved MT-1 under the brand name Scenesse for treating erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), a rare genetic disorder causing extreme photosensitivity. The approved formulation is a subcutaneous implant that releases the peptide over approximately 60 days.

MT-1 offers a "cleaner" profile compared to MT-2, with more selective receptor binding and fewer side effects related to appetite and libido. While both peptides produce skin darkening, MT-1 focuses primarily on melanogenesis without the broader effects on sexual function and appetite that MT-2 produces.

For those seeking tanning benefits with a better safety profile, MT-1 represents a more refined approach to melanocortin-based skin pigmentation.




## How It Works

MT-1 works primarily through activation of the melanocortin 1 receptor (MC1R) on melanocytes, the cells responsible for producing melanin.

**MC1R Activation**

When MT-1 binds to MC1R on melanocytes:

- Melanin synthesis increases significantly
- Melanin is distributed to surrounding keratinocytes (skin cells)
- Skin gradually darkens
- The protective melanin layer reduces UV damage potential

**Selectivity Advantage**

Unlike MT-2 which activates multiple melanocortin receptors (MC1R, MC3R, MC4R, MC5R), MT-1 has higher selectivity for MC1R:

- More focused effect on pigmentation
- Minimal impact on appetite (MC4R related)
- Minimal sexual side effects (MC4R related)
- Cleaner side effect profile overall

**Mechanism of Sun Protection**

The increased melanin provides natural photoprotection:

- Melanin absorbs UV radiation
- Reduces formation of DNA-damaging reactive oxygen species
- Decreases sunburn cell formation
- Provides baseline protection before sun exposure

Clinical studies showed MT-1 treated subjects had 47% fewer sunburn cells compared to controls.

**Pharmacokinetics**

MT-1 has a very short intrinsic half-life (approximately 30 minutes), which is why the approved formulation uses a sustained-release implant. The implant provides an apparent half-life of about 15 hours, with pigmentary effects persisting for weeks after administration.




## Benefits

**Sunless Tanning**

The most established benefit:

- Develops tan with minimal UV exposure
- Deep, natural-looking pigmentation
- Effective for fair-skinned individuals who burn easily
- Tanning effects last weeks after treatment

**UV Protection**

Increased melanin provides natural sun defense:

- Reduced sunburn severity
- Fewer sunburn cells on UV exposure
- Natural "sunscreen" effect
- Synergistic with moderate sun exposure

**Erythropoietic Protoporphyria (EPP) Treatment**

FDA-approved indication:

- Significantly increases pain-free sun exposure time
- Reduces phototoxic reactions
- Improves quality of life dramatically
- Clinical studies show patients can tolerate 6 to 9 months longer in sunlight

**Potential Skin Condition Benefits**

Research suggests possible applications for:

- Vitiligo (repigmentation when combined with light therapy)
- Polymorphic light eruption (reduces severity of reactions)
- Acne vulgaris (some studies show improvement)
- General photoprotection for at-risk individuals

**Cleaner Profile Than MT-2**

Compared to Melanotan 2:

- Minimal impact on appetite
- Minimal sexual side effects
- More predictable effects
- Better safety documentation




## What the Science Shows

MT-1 has the most clinical evidence of any tanning peptide, including FDA approval.

**Arizona Phase 1 Trials (2004)**

Three clinical trials at Arizona Health Sciences Center:

- MT-1 at 0.08 to 0.16 mg/kg daily for 10 days
- Combined with UV exposure
- 47% fewer sunburn cells in treated subjects
- Significantly enhanced tanning compared to controls
- Side effects limited to mild nausea and flushing

**EPP Phase 3 Trials**

The basis for FDA approval:

- 244 adults with EPP across three trials
- 16 mg implant formulation
- Significant increase in pain-free sun exposure
- Quality of life comparable to age-matched healthy population during treatment
- Favorable safety profile

**Vitiligo Studies (2015)**

Multicenter trial combining MT-1 with phototherapy:

- MT-1 implant + NB-UVB phototherapy versus phototherapy alone
- Faster and superior repigmentation with combination
- Particularly effective on face and upper extremities

**Long-Term Safety**

Three-year observational study:

- No serious adverse events
- Sustained benefit over time
- Phototoxic burn tolerance increased 1.8 to 180 fold
- Patients maintain quality of life improvements




## Dosing Protocol

MT-1 dosing differs from MT-2 due to its shorter half-life and the existence of an approved implant formulation.

**Understanding the Dose Context**

Clinical trials used implants releasing sustained doses over 60 days. For injectable formulations, more frequent dosing is required due to the short half-life.

**Injectable Protocol (Research)**

```javascript
Phase           Dose            Frequency           Duration
Loading         0.25 mg         Daily               10 days
Protective      0.25 mg         Before sun exposure As needed
Maintenance     0.25 to 0.5 mg  1 to 2 times weekly Ongoing


```

Note: Due to MT-1's short half-life, effects build with consistent dosing over time.

**Clinical Implant (Scenesse)**

- 16 mg subcutaneous implant
- Administered every 60 days
- Provides consistent release over the period
- Requires medical administration

**Comparison to MT-2**

MT-1 typically requires higher and/or more frequent dosing than MT-2 to achieve similar tanning effects because:

- MT-1 is more selective (focused on MC1R only)
- MT-1 has shorter half-life
- MT-2 is more potent per milligram for tanning

**UV Exposure**

Moderate UV exposure accelerates results:

- 15 to 30 minutes of sun during loading phase
- Less UV needed compared to natural tanning
- Do not burn; the goal is gentle activation

## Reconstitution Instructions

1. Remove the plastic cap from the vial and wipe the rubber stopper with an alcohol swab
2. Draw 2 mL of bacteriostatic water into a sterile syringe
3. Insert the needle through the rubber stopper at an angle
4. Direct the stream of water down the inside wall of the vial
5. Allow the peptide to dissolve without shaking
6. Gently swirl if needed until solution is clear
7. Label the vial with date and concentration (5 mg/mL)

MT-1 dissolves readily. The solution should be clear.




## Side Effects and Cautions

**Common Side Effects**

- Nausea (most common, usually mild and transient)
- Facial flushing (temporary)
- Headache
- Fatigue
- Injection site reactions

**Skin-Related Effects**

- Darkening of existing moles and freckles
- Potential development of new moles
- Overall skin darkening (desired effect)

**Advantages Over MT-2**

MT-1 has a cleaner profile:

- Minimal appetite suppression
- Minimal sexual side effects (no spontaneous erections)
- More predictable, focused effects

**Serious Concerns**

- Unknown long-term effects on melanoma risk (though studies have not shown increased risk)
- Mole changes require monitoring
- Quality concerns with unregulated products




## Who Should Avoid or Use With Care

**Should Avoid:**

- Individuals with history of melanoma or atypical moles
- Those with family history of melanoma
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Anyone with known hypersensitivity to MT-1 or related peptides

**Use With Care:**

- Fair-skinned individuals with many moles (monitor closely)
- Those with autoimmune conditions
- Anyone with liver or kidney impairment




## Success Tips

**Consistency Matters**

Due to MT-1's short half-life, consistent daily dosing during the loading phase is important for building effects. Unlike MT-2 which can be dosed less frequently, MT-1 benefits from regular administration.

**Moderate UV Helps**

While MT-1 can produce some pigmentation without UV, moderate sun exposure accelerates and deepens results. Aim for 15 to 30 minutes of sun during the loading phase.

**Monitor Your Skin**

Document moles before starting. While MT-1 has a good safety profile in clinical studies, any changes in mole appearance should be evaluated by a dermatologist.

**Patience Required**

Effects develop more gradually than with MT-2. Expect visible results over 2 to 4 weeks of consistent use rather than days.




## Injection Technique

1. Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water
2. Gather supplies: reconstituted vial, alcohol swabs, insulin syringe, sharps container
3. Clean the vial stopper with an alcohol swab and allow to air dry
4. Draw the calculated dose into a sterile insulin syringe
5. Clean the injection site (abdomen or thigh) with an alcohol swab
6. Pinch a fold of skin at the injection site
7. Insert the needle at a 45 to 90 degree angle
8. Inject slowly
9. Withdraw the needle and apply light pressure
10. Dispose of the syringe immediately in a sharps container
11. Rotate injection sites




## Storage and Handling

**Before Reconstitution:**

- Protect from light

**After Reconstitution:**

## Legal Status

**United States:** MT-1 (afamelanotide) is FDA approved under the brand name Scenesse for treating erythropoietic protoporphyria. It is a prescription medication for this indication. Sale and use for cosmetic tanning is not FDA approved. Research peptide versions are available.

**International:** Approved in EU, Australia, and other regions for EPP. Regulations vary for cosmetic use.




## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is MT-1 different from MT-2?**

MT-1 is more selective for MC1R (the pigmentation receptor) while MT-2 activates multiple melanocortin receptors. This means MT-1 produces tanning with minimal sexual or appetite effects, while MT-2 has broader systemic effects. MT-1 also has FDA approval and more clinical safety data.

**Is MT-1 safer than MT-2?**

MT-1 has a better documented safety profile due to clinical trials and FDA approval. Its selective receptor binding produces fewer off-target effects. However, both compounds affect melanocytes and share theoretical concerns about mole changes.

**Why do I need to dose MT-1 more frequently than MT-2?**

MT-1 has a much shorter half-life (30 minutes versus several hours for MT-2). The approved formulation uses an implant to provide sustained release. For injectable use, frequent dosing maintains effective levels.

**Can MT-1 cure vitiligo?**

MT-1 has shown promise for vitiligo repigmentation when combined with phototherapy. Clinical trials showed faster and better repigmentation compared to phototherapy alone. It is not a cure but may help restore pigmentation in affected areas.

**Will MT-1 affect my libido?**

Unlike MT-2, MT-1 has minimal effects on sexual function because it does not strongly activate MC4R (the receptor involved in sexual arousal). Most users report no noticeable change in libido.
